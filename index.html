Cosmic Canvas is an AI-powered design tool that bridges the gap between AI creativity and human control.

Most AI image generators produce stunning visuals — but they are static and uneditable. If you want to change a single element, you have to regenerate the entire image.

Cosmic Canvas solves this.

It generates designs using AI and then places them inside an interactive, layer-based canvas editor, allowing users to edit, move, resize, and customize every element individually.

Why Traditional Tools Fall Short

  • Startups often cannot afford dedicated graphic designers.
  • Beginners find tools like Photoshop too complicated to learn.
  • Template tools like Canva limit creativity and flexibility.
  • AI generators produce beautiful images but give users zero control.

The Solution: Our Workflow

Instead of choosing between AI automation and manual design control, Cosmic Canvas gives you both.
